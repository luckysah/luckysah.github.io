About

I'm a Mechatronics Engineering student at Kent State University, in the Honors College with a minor in Computer Science. I like building robots from start to finish: designing the parts in CAD, making them with 3D printing and machining, wiring the electronics, and writing the code that makes them move. Before university, I spent two years building automated production machinery in Nepal.

I'm the Prototyping Manager at the Advanced Telerobotics Research (ATR) Lab and a robotics hardware co-op on the Plaipin AI companion robot. I'm most interested in expressive and humanoid robots, mobile robots and human–robot interaction.

I share my projects here as step-by-step build guides, so others can learn from them and build their own.
